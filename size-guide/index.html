Size guide

M-Mart lists adult shoes in UK sizes, which match Indian sizes. Measure once, then use the charts to find your size or convert from US or EU.

How to measure

  1. 1

    Stand on paper

    Late in the day, wearing the socks you plan to wear, stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall.

  2. 2

    Mark your longest toe

    Keep your weight on the foot and mark the paper at the tip of your longest toe.

  3. 3

    Measure both feet

    Measure from the wall edge to the mark in centimetres. Do both feet and use the longer one.

  4. 4

    Match the chart

    Pick the size whose foot length is the same as yours or just above it. Between two sizes, go up.
